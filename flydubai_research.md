# FlyDubai: research and animation treatment

Research date: 5 October 2026. Development treatment, not a finding from the ongoing investigation.

## Editorial premise

The strongest supported starting point is that Israel received the crew list. Reporting alleges a failure to retrieve and examine crew data. The exact allocation of responsibility is disputed.

Suggested central question: **הרשימה הגיעה. מי בדק אותה?**

Do not build a historical “Shin Bet used to check this feed; Netanyahu switched it off in 2021” sequence on the evidence currently available. A proposed effective screening process can be illustrated, but must be introduced as a proposal rather than a reconstruction of an established former procedure.

Access note: the official guidance and Knesset announcement were available through indexed extracts; full direct retrieval was unavailable. The reporting and ministry response were readable. No original 2021 cabinet decision was located, and the classified Comptroller report was not reviewed.

Three categories must remain distinct: Israeli airline operations, foreign aircraft/crew chartered by Israeli airlines, and foreign carriers operating their own services. FlyDubai belongs to the last category. Neither a report about chartered flights nor an unexplained reference to “2021” establishes the claimed personal decision by Netanyahu about FlyDubai-type flights.

Receipt of identity data does not establish that Israeli authorities already possessed an intelligence warning about this individual. A nationality restriction and evidence of extremist activity are different checks. Do not animate a known terrorist match or a guaranteed prevention outcome without additional evidence.

## Recommended animation: approximately 38 seconds

Use the same project and SVG/timeline/export infrastructure, with a separate future notebook and scene module. This treatment does not create those implementation files yet.

Format: 1080 × 1920 portrait. Keep the lower approximately 500 px clear for captions added during editing. No persistent headline or footer. Short Hebrew labels belong inside institutional cards. Spoken narration should carry the distinctions between proposed procedure, reporting, and the ministry's response.

Keep the airline at upper right, NCPIA at upper left, and a larger assessment station below them. Use the same positions throughout so changes in the connecting arrows carry the story. A faint plane silhouette can move through a separate background route. Institution symbols are sufficient; politician portraits would imply a specific causal intervention that has not been established.

| Time | Picture and movement | Meaning / attribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 s | Airline card releases several crew identity cards. They enter the Tax Authority / מלא״ן database. A small receipt indicator lights up. | Receipt of the actual crew list is confirmed. Highlight crew, not a generic passenger booking. |
| 5–13 s | Narration introduces a proposed process. A copy of a card moves to a magnifier/shield station labelled בדיקה ביטחונית. Two symbolic inputs represent eligibility restrictions and available security information. | Functional proposal: collection → assessment → decision. Do not label the station שב״כ or present it as the documented pre-2021 workflow. |
| 13–18 s | A hypothetical amber flag pauses the card at a review desk; the decision gate waits for review. | A potential issue prompts examination. Amber means “needs review,” not “terrorist.” No suggestion that this exact historical case was guaranteed to match a database. |
| 18–25 s | Return to the same received list. As narration introduces the reporting, its onward arrow fades to a dotted gap. The file remains in the database; the assessment station stays idle. | Illustration of the alleged failure to retrieve/check the data. Do not show an official receiving and destroying it. |
| 25–33 s | A second document, labelled התחייבות החברה, travels from the airline to the Transportation Ministry. A receipt stamp lands on that document while the crew card remains unexamined. | Visual interpretation of the ministry's described reliance on airline assurances. The stamp acknowledges an assurance; it is not individual security clearance. Narration includes the ministry's denial of list access. |
| 33–38 s | Plane silhouette continues. Freeze on the gap between the database and the idle assessment station. | Closing question: הרשימה הגיעה. מי בדק אותה? No conclusion that every possible Israeli check has already been proven absent. |

Timing is provisional: narration may require approximately 40–45 seconds. Each beat should have an adjustable hold in the eventual timeline.

## The garbage-bin idea

An unread file and a disconnected assessment station convey the allegation more precisely. A literal bin would imply the ministry possessed and discarded the list, which is not established. If a bin is eventually used as satire, the narration must identify it as a metaphor for unused information, and it should not replace the disputed account with an apparent documentary sequence.

## Before adding a political handoff scene

Obtain the original 2021 decision or a reliable reproduction identifying its date, scope, and decision-makers. Establish whether the later 2025 decision applies to foreign carriers or only chartered operations. Establish the relevant data-access permissions and the assigned duty to act. Until then, the collected-data / missing-check treatment is ready for storyboard review; the historical handoff remains unresolved.
